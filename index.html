About us

A look at the craft of barbecue.

  • SlowBurn is a small smokehouse run by two friends.
  • We light the fire before sunrise.
  • Oak gives the meat a clean, mellow smoke.
  • We cook in small batches and slice to order.
  • Pick up in Kingston from Friday through Sunday.
